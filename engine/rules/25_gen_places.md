## 25. Making a world — places

List the places the opening needs: where the player starts and the nearby places the first days touch. 3–10, no more.

```text
id        lowercase words joined by underscores
surface   what anyone sees on arrival; feature: one more concrete detail
band      the range of challenge that can plausibly be found there (1–20) and why. A quiet shop is 1–4; a warren under the city is not.
          band_min <= band_max.
start_location is one of the ids. The player's starting_activity must be possible there.
```
