## 26. Making a world — people and groups

Three or more people who live here whether or not the player ever meets them; each wants something and has somewhere to be.

```text
NAME     a FULL name (given + family, or the setting's full form). Never a first name alone.
GENDER   a plain word. Never blank.
POSITION a place id from the places list.
PLAN     what they are doing; DUE: when it next changes, written exactly "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM what happens" on or after the start,
  or "when <condition>" if it waits on an event. Every person has one. Nobody waits for the player.
TIE      how they stand to the player now; ATTITUDE a few plain words. A first meeting is neutral, never friendly by default.
WORK SOURCE  a fixer, guild, employer or patron who routes work to the player: set work_source to how often work could come ("weekly" if unsure); everyone else "".
LEVEL    only for people who can fight; set it so a fight with them is believable at their job.
FACTIONS name, role, what they want and fear. Only groups that act in the story.
```
