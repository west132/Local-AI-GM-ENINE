## 20. Ten-round check

Every tenth round, look at what is open and close what the records show is settled. Nothing else.

```text
CLOSE  a quest whose objective is met, failed or abandoned · a right whose terms are done · a payoff that was
  delivered · a thread that has run its course: each as its own change, with the record that shows it (because)
LEAVE  anything still live; say in `notes` why you left it open
NEVER  open something new, invent a fact, or close a thing because it is old or quiet
```
