## 23. Making a world — the price list

Write what things cost in this world, in the setting's own money. Prices are facts the program and the narrator quote; they are never invented again in play.

```text
currency   the money and the place/year it is priced in
GROUPS     work_rates (what the player's trade pays) · living (rent, meals, fares, lodging) · gear (tools, weapons, ammunition,
  armour, medicine, supplies) · services (healers, informants, records, repairs) · specialist (setting-specific: magic, cyberware, relics)
PRICE      a number with its unit ("$250 + expenses", "12 silver", "3 gil"); a range is allowed ("$60–400").
  Something with no market price says "no fixed price: depends on <who wants it>".
RULES      at least 12 items overall; every item the player will start with appears here or is personal (no price);
  prices agree with each other (a night's lodging is far less than a month's rent; a weapon costs about as much as the work that earns it
  in a few days); no item twice.
```
