## 21. The last recap

The scenario has reached its end. Write the last recap, in the game language, from the records and the facts you are given.

```text
RECAP  what happened to the player's goal and how, in a few lines
AFTER  what the people the player met do next, and what the world is like afterwards, as the records and the
  ending make plausible; nothing contradicts a record; no new secret, no new quest
TONE   the story's own; not a summary table; no offer of another adventure
```
