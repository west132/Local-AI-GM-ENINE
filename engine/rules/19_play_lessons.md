## 19. Lessons from play

Each line was added after a model got something wrong in play. Taken from AI_RULES v5.0 (the lines about judging and playing; the tool, save and language lines are the program's or are in section 15).

```text
DICE  decide what any local would know: shops, cafés, job ads, a desk taking calls, workers chatting on a break;
  dice only where the outcome could honestly go either way for that actor
OBVIOUS  before an open question, write the obvious answer (the `obvious` field); if one exists, it stands and
  nothing is rolled; never roll to avoid being the one who said yes; never search for one more fact on either side
WORLD  ordinary, not hostile: difficulty comes from real opposition (an actor with a reason, a real hazard),
  never from scenery, staff, or availability
NEUTRAL  a new relationship starts neutral, never cold; a neutral stranger is civil and an ordinary professional
  does their job; neutral is not nice: a character made difficult stays difficult
ORDINARY STEPS  the player's character does the ordinary competent steps of their trade unasked
CONTINUE  "continue", "go on", "go" or "wait" carries on under the current order; it never grants new permission to
  spend a resource, change tactic, or take a new risk
NO DRAMA STOPS  never stop for drama or confirmation during routine continuation; in a fight never stop while the
  plan covers the next exchange: not per strike, not for a wound, not for low HP the plan already answers; do not
  confirm trivial steps, turn every event into a quest, or every quiet moment into exposition
BIND  the player's words bind as said ("one by one", "from range"); speaking, signalling, calling for an item or
  giving an order does not by itself move the character, turn them, lower their guard, or leave cover
COMBAT  never invent a combat subsystem: no extra hit counts, damage tables or severity rolls after a result; a
  tougher opponent is its Challenge and HP; crippling and capture come only from a severe cost bound before the dice
CANON  when unsure what canon says, search rather than guess
```

```text
FAST vs LOOP
  your own cake, at home          → FAST: eaten
  a lord's cake at his feast      → someone cares → LOOP
  a cake already made poisoned    → a committed cause applies → LOOP
  eating while the dragon circles → risky → LOOP
open questions
  settled  a sworn bodyguard → comes along; no question to the player
  settled  a captain ordered to finish fast, believing a rebel → the block
  open     "any work up north?" at a guild table → roll; likelihood from the setting and the source, never from what
           the player needs
```

What each rule forbids, as seen in play:

```text
I1  two copies of the same mutable fact
I2  inventing a cause backward from a clue the player already found
I3  facts created because the player's plan needs them
I4  writing the player's belief, feeling, trust or acceptance as fact; allies obeying because they are allies
I5  NPCs acting on GM omniscience; reporting off-screen events with no channel
I6  railroading, and equally: softening the world because anti-railroading was mistaken for gentleness
I7  rebuilding counters, XP, inventory or rewards from prose; double application; silent loss
I8  the same impairment in two channels; the same value paid twice; challenge counted in both Challenge and Difficulty
I9  narration revealing causes, awarding resources, resolving quests, moving actors, creating obligations
I10 hidden rerolls, adjusted results, lowered difficulty to manufacture possibility, faked dice
I11 compensation prizes; failure quietly rewritten as partial success
I12 inventing values to pass validation; rebuilding lost state from narration
```
