I take Nadia's case at $700 flat, $350 up front. I ask her to show me the phone locator and I keep Eli's map with the Red Spur circled.
Before I head out I phone Pike and tell him I'll do the Suds & Spin basement job tomorrow morning. Then I ride to the Canal Street embankment where Eli's phone was last seen.
I look over the embankment and hut 3 for signs. The creature watching me from the weeds, I attack and kill it.
I knock on hut 3's wedged door and tell whoever is inside that Nadia Voss sent me to find Eli.
I hear Eli out, ask to see the shard he took, and tell him the shard is what draws the creatures.
I get Eli out of there and take him back to Hale Workshop through the traffic.
At the workshop I try to sense the shard's resonance through plastic, cardboard, glass and a steel tin, then leave it in the tin on the shelf.
I call Nadia in, tell her Eli is safe, and take the $350 balance in cash.
I agree Eli stays on my sofa tonight and promise Nadia I'll take him to a no-questions clinic tomorrow. Nadia goes home by cab.
I lock up and we sleep the night at the workshop.
